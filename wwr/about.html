Rufiji Rapids Rafting

We exist to share the thrill of the river safely, with local guides who love where they work and guests who leave wanting more.

History

Rufiji Rapids began with two friends, one worn-out raft, and a stretch of river nobody else wanted to run. Today our guides lead hundreds of guests a season, and we still check every life jacket ourselves.
